## What it knows

Vefsafn is the web archive of the National and University Library of Iceland. Small next to Wayback, but it keeps timestamps and costs nothing to ask, so there's no reason to skip it.

## How it asks

One request to `/cdx` with `matchType=domain`, `output=json` and `fields=url,timestamp`. NDJSON, streamed.

## Traps

- It ignores `limit` and streams the whole domain dump. The provider reads lines until the collector has enough, then cancels the reader, so a `limit: 20` still ends after 20 kept URLs and not after the dump.
- `output=txt` is rejected, same as Arquivo.pt.
