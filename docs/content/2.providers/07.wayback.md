## What it knows

Everything the Internet Archive captured under the domain since 1996. For an old domain that's the deepest source by far, and every row has a timestamp.

## How it asks

One request to `/cdx/search/cdx` with `matchType=domain`, `output=txt` and `fl=original,timestamp`. The answer streams line by line and the reader is cancelled the moment the collector has enough.

## Traps

- `url=domain/*` only matches the apex host, `www.` included. Subdomains never came back that way, so the provider asks with `matchType=domain`, like urlfinder's `*.domain/*`.
- `collapse=urlkey` would dedupe on the server, but that variant times out from some networks while the plain query answers at once. The collector dedupes instead.
- Rows are captures, not URLs. `http://example.com:80/` captured in 2002 and `http://example.com/` captured in 2020 are one URL after dedup. The first spelling wins and the dates widen.
- On a big domain the full dump is huge. Set a `limit` or a filter, or run your own CDX server and point `baseUrl` at it.
